Patterns that show up in our studies

We've seen these across existing clients and expect to see them in future ones. How big each one is varies by organization, which is why every engagement starts with a study.

Topic affinity

Supporters respond more when the ask is on their topic

1.6x to 2.2xthe gift rate when a donor's ask is aligned to their top topic
Clients A and B
Generic ask1x
Aligned ask1.6–2.2x
  • Supporters who haven't given yet engage more with aligned emails: 8 points more opens at Client A, and 25 more clicks per 1,000 sends at Client C.
  • Framing counts too. At Client A, political framing was safe for politically engaged supporters and saw 29% more unsubscribes from everyone else.
Ladder of engagement

Interest fades fast, so timing matters

7.7xas likely to give a first gift the month after a never-giver takes an action
Client C

4 to 6 months: no lift

  • At Client A, asks on a topic someone engaged with in the last 60 days saw 57% more gifts than asks on older interest.
  • At Client C, one-time donors were 3.1x as likely to start a sustainer plan in the 3 months after a gift.

How we put the patterns to work

Every organization is different. These are the interventions we run with clients today, and the three the calculator counts.

Topic routing

Write a few more versions of the ask, and send each supporter the one closest to what they care about.

Generic ask1x
Ask on the donor's topic1.6–2.2x
In your calculator
What it looks like
For tentpole moments like year-end and Giving Tuesday, the team writes 3 to 10 versions of the ask. Chorus splits the list so each supporter gets the closest one.
How we measure it
Increase in gift rate on the sends that use it, against a holdout that gets the standard ask.

Strategic suppression

Keep your own business rules, and hold back the people unlikely to give and likely to leave.

What it looks like
For any send, you choose the audience with your own rules. Chorus returns a suppression file. List health improves right away, and over time you keep a larger list and more future gifts.
How we measure it
Unsubscribes avoided, valued at the future giving each supporter would have brought in. On SMS we also count the money saved by sending fewer texts.

How an engagement works

Step 1

Study

Every program is different. The study finds which of these patterns matter most in your data and sizes each one.

Step 2

Pilot

We run the top intervention against a holdout for one to two months, so you see your own lift before you commit.

Step 3

Orchestrate

We add the top interventions one at a time so your team isn't overwhelmed. $10 per 1,000 supporter records a month, across email, SMS, and other channels.

Figures come from Chorus audience studies completed in 2026, with clients described by type. Findings are associations measured on each organization's historical sends, except the match test, which was randomized. Client C's figures are in Canadian dollars. The calculator is an estimate for planning; a study replaces each default with values measured in your own data.
